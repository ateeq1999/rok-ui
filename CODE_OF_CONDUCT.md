# Code of Conduct

rok-ui follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to everyone taking part in the project: in issues, pull requests, discussions and
any other project space.

In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming, including to people who are new to Rust or GPUI.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks, or sexualized language or imagery.
- No publishing anyone's private information without their permission.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer,
[@ateeq1999](https://github.com/ateeq1999), privately. Reports are kept confidential. The
maintainer will review each report and respond with the enforcement steps the Contributor
Covenant describes: correction, warning, temporary ban or permanent ban, depending on the
impact of the behaviour.
